# Experiment 02 — News Verification and Source Audit

## 1. Objective

Evaluate whether Hermes Agent can review the five news items collected in Experiment 01, compare them with available sources, identify uncertainty, and recommend stories for further editorial review.

## 2. Verification Tasks

Hermes was asked to:

- Check the URLs provided in the initial collection.
- Compare headlines, publication dates, and article contents.
- Distinguish original sources from secondary reporting.
- Look for supporting evidence where available.
- Assign a verification status.
- Recommend potential stories for a reflective Substack essay.

## 3. Initial Findings

Based on the audit output reviewed during this experiment:

- The Associated Press item was reported to have content consistent with the article, although the headline differed slightly.
- The Reuters report was accessed through Investing.com, which was identified as the publishing platform for the Reuters report.
- The audit distinguished company announcements from independent evaluations of their claims.
- The GPT-Rosalind announcement was identified as a primary source for OpenAI's own program details, not independent evidence of its scientific impact.
- The audit recommended treating allegations in the OpenAI lawsuit as allegations rather than established legal findings.

These observations reflect the audit output shown in the experiment. They do not establish that every claim has been independently verified.

## 4. Important Correction

Hermes acknowledged that the publication dates previously presented for the Associated Press and Intel items had been stated with more certainty than the accessible source evidence justified.

This illustrates why publication dates should be recorded with their evidence and limitations.

## 5. Editorial Recommendations

The audit suggested several possible essay directions:

1. International AI governance and oversight.
2. AI adoption among business executives.
3. AI infrastructure and model development.
4. Access to AI tools for scientific research.
5. Legal disputes and accountability in AI safety.

The Reuters-related topic was recommended as the leading candidate in the audit output, subject to further source checking.

## 6. Limitations

The complete set of five articles has not yet been independently rechecked by the project author.

A source being accessible does not prove every statement in an article. Company announcements should also be distinguished from independent evidence of effectiveness or impact.

## 7. Lessons Learned

- AI-generated research requires evidence-based review.
- Original sources and secondary reporting serve different purposes.
- Dates and headlines must not be presented with unjustified certainty.
- Legal allegations must not be described as proven facts.
- Human editorial judgment remains essential.

## 8. Status

**Initial audit completed; independent verification remains ongoing.**

## 9. Next Step

Select one candidate story, independently verify its key claims, and develop a reflective essay outline before drafting the full essay.
