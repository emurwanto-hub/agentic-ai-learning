# Experiment 01 — International News Collection

## 1. Objective

Test whether Hermes Agent can collect and summarize international news about artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

## 2. Prompt

The agent was asked to collect five recent international news stories and provide:

- Headline
- Original media or source
- Publication date, if available
- Source URL
- Short summary in Indonesian
- Explanation of why the story matters

The agent was instructed not to invent news, dates, or URLs and to disclose verification limitations.

## 3. Initial Results

Hermes Agent returned five news items covering:

1. A lawsuit involving OpenAI and allegations concerning chatbot safety.
2. A proposal for an international body to oversee advanced AI.
3. An IBM survey concerning AI adoption among executives.
4. Intel's reported support for Meta's Muse Spark model.
5. OpenAI's GPT-Rosalind Academic Plan for research laboratories.

The agent presented the results in a table with summaries and explanations of their potential significance.

## 4. Initial Observations

- The output followed the requested structure.
- The summaries were written in Indonesian.
- The agent identified sources from news organizations and company newsrooms.
- Some publication dates and claims required additional scrutiny.
- A structured answer alone does not establish factual accuracy.

## 5. Limitations

At this stage, the five stories had not all been independently verified.

The initial results should be treated as candidate news items rather than fully approved material for publication.

## 6. Lessons Learned

- Clear prompts help produce consistent output.
- Source URLs and publication dates should be checked.
- Original sources should be distinguished from syndicated or secondary reporting.
- Human review remains necessary before selecting news for an essay.

## 7. Status

**Initial collection completed.** Further verification was conducted in Experiment 02.

## 8. Next Step

Audit the five news items, check the available evidence, document corrections, and identify stories suitable for a reflective Substack essay.
