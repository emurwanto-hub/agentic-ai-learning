# International News Curator

## Project Overview

International News Curator is a learning project to explore how an AI agent can collect, evaluate, and curate international news about artificial intelligence and emerging technologies.

The project uses Hermes Agent to support news research, source verification, and the preparation of reflective essays for Substack.

The goal is not only to produce content, but also to develop a reliable and transparent AI-assisted research workflow.

## Objectives

- Collect relevant international news about AI and technology.
- Identify original sources and distinguish them from secondary reporting.
- Verify important claims against accessible evidence.
- Evaluate the relevance and reliability of potential stories.
- Turn selected news into reflective essays in Indonesian.
- Document experiments, prompts, results, and lessons learned.

## Tools

- **Hermes Agent:** AI agent for research and workflow experiments.
- **ChatGPT Go / Codex:** Existing AI access, subject to usage limits.
- **GitHub:** Project documentation and learning portfolio.
- **Substack:** Intended publication platform for reflective essays.

## Workflow

1. Collect international news.
2. Inspect original sources and publication dates.
3. Verify claims and record limitations.
4. Select a story for further analysis.
5. Draft a reflective essay in Indonesian.
6. Review the draft before any publication.
7. Document the process and lessons learned.

## Verification Principles

- Never invent sources, URLs, publication dates, or evidence.
- Distinguish allegations from established facts.
- Clearly disclose uncertainty and access limitations.
- Do not treat an accessible URL as proof that every claim is true.
- Keep human review and editorial approval in the workflow.

## Current Limitations

The initial experiments have not yet been independently verified in full. News selection and factual claims must be reviewed before being used in a published essay.

## Learning Approach

This repository documents the learning process, including successful experiments, limitations, corrections, and lessons learned.

The project will evolve incrementally, with a focus on reliability, transparency, and practical understanding of agentic AI.
